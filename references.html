How this site uses sources

Prepared by Julia Mathew for a course website project. Research was reviewed on October 25, 2026. Source numbers link to the organizations’ original publications. Research findings are paraphrased; hypothetical household, classroom, and workplace examples are identified as illustrative.

The Pew study reports survey attitudes, the IMF analysis examines economic and care patterns, and Swedish guidance describes a benefits system. These sources answer different questions and do not provide directly comparable national scores. None represents every person’s lived experience.

The OECD and UNESCO sources support discussion of education and media. Much of the research uses binary gender categories; this limits what can be concluded about transgender and nonbinary experiences.
